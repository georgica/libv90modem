# V.92 Quick Connect (answering) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** libv90modem 0.7.4 gives a V.92 caller that asks for Quick Connect
(QC1a in our ANSam, V.92 9.2.4.1) the short Phase 1 and the short Phase 2
(9.4.1) on any port that offers `V90M_MOD_V92`, falls back to ANSam/V.8
within V.92's 2 s when the caller does not follow, acknowledges a V.8bis MS
that leaves the modulation to V.8, and reports what happened through a new
`v90m_get_qc()`. A caller that sends no QC1a sees exactly 0.7.3.

**Architecture:**
- QC1a is heard by the V.8 receiver we already run during ANSam: a QC tracker
  beside the CM assembler in `modem/v8rx` (`v8rx_tick_qc`), fed the same
  characters, writing only QC state. `v8ans` ends with `V8A_RES_QC` when QC
  is offered.
- The digital short Phase 1 (QCA1d, 75 ms, QTS, QTS\, ANSpcm, TONEq wait,
  75 ms) is a new engine `qcd_t` (`modem/qcd.[ch]`) in the answerer's union
  (`answer_t.u.qc`), run by a new phase `ANS_QC` (scheme `ans_qc`,
  `modem/ans_qc.c`, codeword output). Signals and tables: `modem/qc.[ch]`,
  `modem/qc_tab.c` (generated by `tools/gen_anspcm.py`).
- What must outlive the engine (flags, the QC1a octet, the 6-symbol frame
  grid counter, the TONEq time) is an 8-octet record `v8a_qc_t` carved out of
  the tail of `v8ans_t.jmbits` (our JM never needs more than 15 octets): no
  struct grows on any target, padding is not relied on.
- The grid from QTS's first symbol (8.3.6) reaches Phases 3 and 4 through
  `v90tx_align()`, a new call on the V.90 transmitter that puts its 6-symbol
  grid in place before its first selection (`v90/v90_tx`).
- Short Phase 2 is a branch of `p2d` (states `P2D_SHORT_*`), requested in
  INFO0d bit 26 only in calls that came through short Phase 1; it recovers
  to the full Phase 2 that already exists. Table 19's INFO1a reaches
  `start_p34` with its carrier bit.
- V.8bis: `v8bis_ms_v8()` recognises a valid MS for data with "V.8 follows"
  and no K56flex block; such an MS asking for ACK(1) gets it.

**Tech Stack:** C99 fixed point, GNU make, POSIX sh, Python 3 (the ANSpcm
table generator), Bootlin cross toolchains with qemu-arm-static (size checks
only); BinModem (Rust, outside the repository) as a black-box live peer.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-v92-qc-design.md` (cited as

## Review Focus

1. **A false QC1a in a caller that sends none** (CM only, our ANSam's echo, line noise, a pre-V.8 caller's characters). A QC1a needs, twice, ≥ 8 mark bits, the character 0x55, exactly one information character with bits 0, 1 and 4 clear, then ≥ 8 marks; both information characters equal. Test (Task 3, `test_qc1a`): 24 CM-only callers (every CM our tests use, levels −9 to −43 dBm0), each run with QC offered and not offered → the transmitted sample streams identical, `V8A_QC_HEARD` clear; ANSam echoed at −6 dB with a 5 ms delay under a CM → identical; 5 s of noise at −30 and −46 dBm0, 20 seeds → no QC, `V8A_RES_NO_CM` on the same sample as not offered; start-stop text `UDUDUD…` with one stop bit between characters → no QC; QC1d (bit 0 set) twice, one copy only, two different copies, an extra character after the information character → no QC, the CM decides. The one pattern that is a QC1a by Table 2 (`U`,`D`, ten marks, `U`,`D`) is pinned as heard, so the residue is documented.
2. **TONEq never comes, or comes late.** (a) The caller hears QCA1d and never sends TONEq; (b) it misses QCA1d and keeps sending CM (its 980 Hz marks must not pass for TONEq); (c) a long round trip (TONEq begins 1.90 s after QCA1d at our port) and a too-long one (2.10 s). Test (Task 5, `test_answer_qc` `no_toneq`, `deaf`, `late_toneq`, `too_late`): (a), (b) and the 2.10 s case: ANSam again exactly 16000 samples after QCA1d's last sample, then V.8 (JM to the caller's CM) and V.92 data, `fallback` set; (c) accepted, data. No TONEq is detected in (b).
3. **The caller's stored parameters are stale (the line changed).** Short Phase 2 must recover, and a QC start-up that fails must not cost the call its PCM upstream. Test (Task 6 `test_p2_qc`, Task 7 `test_answer_qc`): no Tone A reversal within 2500 ms → full Phase 2 with INFO1d (Table 17) → done; no INFO1a within 2500 ms → full Phase 2; Table 19 naming 3429 (we do not receive it) → full Phase 2; a QC + short Phase 2 + DIL N = 0 start-up whose PCM upstream sends nothing (`mute_pcm` 1) → our retrain, full Phase 2 with INFO1d bit 70 still 1, V.92 data; with `mute_pcm` 2 → bit 70 cleared after the second failure, V.90 data (0.7.3's rule).
4. **QC after our V.8bis exchange (K56flex on).** Test (Task 8, `test_answer_qc` `k56_no_crd`, `k56_ms_v8`, `k56_agreed`): a QC caller that ignores CRe gets ANSam on the very sample a caller without QC1a gets it, then QC, V.92 data; a QC caller that answers our CL with the MS `11 89 80 80 81 c2` gets ACK(1), ANSam, QC, data; a K56flex client that was agreed never meets QC (`qc_aux` offered bit cleared at agreement) and its run is unchanged (`test_answer_k56` golden untouched).
5. **An A-law line.** QTS and ANSpcm are law-specific codewords and our K56flex/x2 offers are µ-law only. Test (Task 2 `test_qcsig`: Tables 7–10 A-law columns, QTS A-law octets; Task 5 `test_answer_qc` `alaw`; Task 7 `alaw_short`): an A-law QC caller (with and without short Phase 2) reaches V.92 data, the sim checks every QTS/ANSpcm codeword against its own A-law generator, Sd lands on QTS's grid.

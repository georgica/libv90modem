# K56flex answering side: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** libv90modem answers a K56flex call as the digital (server) side:
V.8bis as the initiator, V.34 Phase 2 with K56flex flagged in INFO1a, the
server's PCM training, downstream data at 32000 to 56000 bit/s (pads 0, 3,
6 dB, robbed bits), the client's renegotiations and retrains. A caller that
does not take K56flex up gets exactly what it gets today.

**Architecture:**
- Offering K56flex adds a Phase 1 in front of V.8 (`modem/v8bis`, its
  messages in `modem/v8bismsg`): MRe, CRd, our CL, the client's MS, ACK(1).
  An agreement turns x2 off for the call and makes V.34's `p2ans` flag
  INFO1a (frequency offset `0x001`) when INFO1c carries `0x001` and the
  upstream is 3200 baud.
- After the flagged INFO1a a new engine, `modem/k56d`, plays the part that
  `x2d` plays for x2: one received linear sample in, one mu-law octet out.
  - transmit: `k56tx` (the training script, MP, MP', E) and `k56enc`
    (frames of 8 symbols through V.34's shell mapper, `k56tab`'s rings and
    magnitudes);
  - receive: the client's V.34 upstream (S ... J24, J', TRN, MP, E, data)
    through `qamrx` + `v34/`, copied from x2d's few lines (x2d untouched).
- D-Modem (AGPL) and Motorola's `sm56.ko` are black boxes outside the
  repository. Their output is pinned in the tests as data our code must
  equal.

**Tech stack:** C99, fixed point, the existing `modem/`, `v34/`, `v90/`,
`sim/` code, make; Python 3 for the table generator.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-k56flex-answering-design.md`.
Research: `~/modem-research/reports/x2 and K56flex protocols.md`,
`~/modem-research/research_notes/x2 and K56flex protocols/k56flex_technical.md`,
`~/modem-research/sources/dmodem-k56flex-details.md` (D-Modem's notes, a
copy). Template: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-20-x2-answering.md` and
`docs/history/x2-facts.md`.

**Worktree:** `~/k56-wt`, branch `k56flex` (from `main` at 704b370, 0.4.0
plus the x2 gate fix). Never touch `~/v90d` or `~/x2-wt`.

## Global Constraints

- C99, fixed point in the signal path, no allocation after create, no
  global state, identical output on every port target (fixed-width types,
  no left shift of negative numbers, no byte-order assumptions, PRId64).
- Test-first: each test is seen failing before the code that passes it.
- GPL-2.0-only. **Nothing is copied from D-Modem or courier-emu as code or
  prose.** Facts may be taken from any source, each cited by URL and commit
  and tagged in `docs/history/k56-facts.md` (documented, RE (D-Modem), our
  observation, derived). No SM56 binary, firmware or recording in the
  repository.
- **Tables: "Rebuild first, else include."** Derive where a rule reproduces
  the table; otherwise `modem/k56tab.c`, generated by
  `tools/gen_k56_tables.py` from D-Modem's published values, naming its
  origin, listed in `docs/limits.md`.
- `V90M_MOD_K56` (0x0200): answering only, needs `V90M_MOD_V34`, mu-law
  only, not in the defaults.
- After the point of no return (our flagged INFO1a) every failure clears
  down with `V90M_END_K56_FAILED`; every wait is bounded, each under 10 s.
- K56flex not offered: output byte-identical to 0.4.0 (replay proof).
- `v90m_mem_size()` stays 118824 on x86-64 and 118512 on ARMv5;
  `V90M_MEM_SIZE` unchanged.
- CPU: K56flex data mode at most 1.2x V.90 answering's 38.9 M ARM
  instructions a second (`make cost-k56`).
- D-Modem is cloned at `c06a380d` into `~/modem-research/D-Modem` and built
  and run there, unmodified. Oracle drivers, the SM56 decoder harness and
  Gough Lui's recording live under `~/modem-research/`, never in the repo.
- The SM56 harnesses need `glibc-devel.i686` (not installed on 2026-11-21;
  `libgcc.i686` is). The owner installs it. A step that needs it and finds
  it missing records "skipped: glibc-devel.i686 not installed" in

## Review Focus

1. **A V.8bis caller without K56flex** (a V.92 modem: CRd, then no MS):
   V.8 starts 1500 ms after our CL ends and the call connects as before.
   Test in Task 5: `k56call` with `v8bis = K56C_V8BIS_NO_MS`.
2. **An agreed caller whose CM offers PCM while we offer V.90:** JM selects
   V.90, as today; no INFO1 flag, no x2 JM flip. Test in Task 5.
3. **An agreed caller with INFO1c not `0x001`, or an upstream we select at
   3000 baud:** plain V.34, unflagged, the offset nudged off `..01`. Test in
   Task 6.
4. **The client going silent after our flagged INFO1a** (before its S,
   before J, before MP, before E, in data): `V90M_END_K56_FAILED` (data:
   `V90M_END_LOST`) within 10 s, never stuck. Test in Task 9.
5. **A J octet with pad code 3, or an MP with `rate_ac` 0, mask 0, or no
   upstream rate we receive:** a clean clear-down with
   `V90M_END_K56_FAILED`, no crash. Test in Task 9.

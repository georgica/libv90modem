# Development history

These are the development logs of libv90modem: `PROGRESS.md` (what was
built, tested and measured, step by step, with the test output),
`V34-REPORT.md` (the report on V.34 answering), `x2-facts.md` and
`k56-facts.md` (what x2 and K56flex are built from, each fact with its
source), and `k56-handover.md` (K56flex's line tests still to make, and
what each is to answer). They are kept as history
and are not maintained documentation: paths, counts and statements in them
are as they were on the day each section was written. The maintained
documents are in the parent directory, `docs/`.
